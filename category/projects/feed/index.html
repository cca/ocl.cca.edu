Students from Queering Kinship will generate data about unique kinship networks for Visualizing Bodies of Information students to evaluate and visualize using the Processing programming language. Co-working times and asynchronous chat environments will be used to facilitate dialogue and inspiration between the classes. Stay tuned for an end of semester exhibition!
